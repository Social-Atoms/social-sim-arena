## Choices the agent made (can be overturned)

- Owner update 2026-11-10: remove the unscored trial requirement and prepare production immediately. Data validity and baseline gates remain mandatory.
- Question chosen without asking: Downtown Core at Friday 23:00, the doc's first proposal. Reason: it is the city's nightlife and office core, the second-largest pool after the Changi airport queue, and its count moves more than its noise. Runner-up: Changi, but that mostly measures the airport queue, which follows flight schedules.
- Validity rule: 30% of the 4-week median (the doc's figure) plus a staleness limit of 120 s. A 50% threshold was tested and wrongly rejected 2026-01-23 (2,251 against a median inflated by the high-count period), so 30% was kept. 120 s is about twice the largest healthy lag seen (65 s). Easy to change.
- Resolution capture at Friday 23:30 SGT instead of at the minute itself. Reason: the endpoint serves past minutes, so a short delay costs nothing and avoids racing the feed. Runner-up: live poll at 23:00 plus the date_time fetch as a check.
- Suggested arena names: tracker 'sg_lta_rt'; series 'sg_taxi_free_downtown_fri2300'; adapter ssa/adapters/sg_taxi.py; archive sources/lta-rt/taxi/; round_id 'sg-taxi-dtc-<YYYY-MM-DD>'. Naming only.

## Target

- **Population** — Taxis that LTA's feed reports as available for hire (not 'Hired' or 'Busy') at the snapshot, with a reported position inside the DOWNTOWN CORE polygon. Private-hire cars (e.g. Grab) are not in the feed [owner doc; LTA guide says the feed covers taxis].
- **Window** — One instant: Friday 23:00:00 Singapore time, read as the snapshot the API returns for date_time=<Friday>T23:00:00, subject to the validity rules below.
- **Timezone** — Asia/Singapore
- **Calculation** — Metric definition v1, committed with a timestamp before the first window. (1) Request date_time=<target>T23:00:00. (2) The snapshot is valid if properties.timestamp is no more than 120 s before 23:00:00 and properties.taxi_count is at least 30% of the median island-wide count of the last 4 valid Friday 23:00 snapshots in SSA's archive. (3) If invalid, request 23:01, 23:02, 23:05 and 23:10 in turn, each judged by the same rule against its own minute; the first valid one is used. (4) Count the points in geometry.coordinates that fall inside the DOWNTOWN CORE polygon (ray casting, holes excluded) of the pinned boundary file. A point on a boundary goes to the first matching polygon in file order. Measured: island-wide valid snapshots had timestamps 0-65 s before the minute.
- **Unit** — Free taxis (count of points)

## Lock, scoring and resolution

- **Lock** — Wednesday 23:00 Singapore time (15:00Z), 48 hours before the target minute, as in the doc. The behaviour has not happened at lock. Owner update 2026-11-10: no unscored trial requirement. Every admitted future round is scored, subject to the existing reference, capture and cancellation rules. First proposed scored lock: 2026-12-09T15:00:00Z. Publish on main before lock; a missing or invalid reference does not become a fabricated baseline.
- **Baseline** — Ranked zero point mean_k(k=4, lag=0, rms_n=13) over valid Friday 23:00 snapshots (owner decision, rev 2; rev 1 called it 'slot_mean4'): mean of the Downtown Core counts in the last 4 valid Friday 23:00 snapshots before the lock, with sd = the root-mean-square of that same rule's errors over the 13 most recent valid target Fridays, all from SSA's archive at lock. Zero-point family (G1): one parameterised reference 'mean_k(k, lag, rms_n)': the mean of the k most recent eligible values in the lock capture, after skipping 'lag' newer ones not yet published, with sd = the root-mean-square of that same rule's errors over the rms_n most recent resolved targets in the lock capture (G2). Persistence is mean_k(k=1). Measured on 63 valid Fridays 2025-08-08 to 2026-11-06 (data.gov.sg past-minute snapshots fetched 2026-11-09): last 26 weeks, mean 162.5 taxis: 4-week mean MAE 44.8 (28%), 4-week median 45.4 (28%), last week (doc's persistence) 57.7 (35%). Over all 59 scorable weeks: 76.8 vs 77.1. Spread check over the last 26 weeks (lesson 12): mean_k(k=4) with the 13-week RMS: CRPS 34.0, best rescale gain +0.8% (k 0.80), 90% coverage 0.88; persistence with its own 13-week RMS: CRPS 39.8, +0.5%, 0.96; with the arena's fixed sd 1.5: 44.0 and 56.9, so a copy of the reference with an honest spread would gain +23% to +30%. Shocks, error of the 4-week mean then persistence: 2025-10-03, the Friday of the Singapore F1 weekend [event date inferred, not checked] (440 free) +324 / +341; 2025-12-26 (538) +382 / +310; 2026-01-02 (586) +322 / +48; Chinese New Year week 2026-02-20 (240) +116 / +46; Good Friday 2026-04-03 (238) +66 / +130. Holidays raise free taxis downtown (offices closed), which is the response the question is meant to reward. Shown, not ranked: persistence (same minute a week earlier).
- **Resolution** — SSA fetches the target minute (and, if needed, the fallback minutes) once at Friday 23:30 SGT and archives every body fetched, valid or not. If the 23:30 run fails to connect, one more attempt at Saturday 01:00 SGT; the endpoint serves past minutes, so the later fetch reads the same snapshot. The resolved value is the count from the first valid snapshot under the calculation rule. The archive, not the live endpoint, is the record; later fetches never change a resolved round. The round text states that SSA is both the question setter and the resolver.
- **Missing data policy** — (1) No valid snapshot among 23:00, 23:01, 23:02, 23:05 and 23:10 (owner decision, rev 2): the round is cancelled, never resolved from another hour. Measured: of 66 Friday 23:00 snapshots, three were glitched: 2026-07-17 (439 island-wide; a partial feed from about 22:00 to after 23:55), 2026-08-14 (260, the feed frozen at 22:46:24 for over an hour) and 2026-10-16 (95, low from before 21:00 to about 23:58). None recovered within 10 minutes, so all three rounds would have been cancelled: 4.5% of 66 weeks, but 3 of the last 17 (18%). (2) Fewer than 4 valid Fridays among the last 6 in the archive, for the reference or the validity median: the round is not posted. (3) If URA republishes the boundary file, the pinned hash stays for the season. (4) If the feed's schema changes (properties renamed, points missing), the round is cancelled and the card revised. (5) Historical comparisons use past-minute snapshots fetched later, which are labelled as such.
- **Scoring** — SSA topline scoring: mean and sd (sd > 0) or a quantile map including 0.5, CRPS; arena target_type continuous_normal. Skill = 1 - CRPS(entrant) / CRPS(mean_k(k=4, rms_n=13)). arena scoring.noise_floor on the Downtown Core count, last 52 valid weeks: m 31.5, s 83.4, so movement exceeds noise and the series is scorable. For comparison, Downtown Core's share of the island (not used, owner decision rev 2): m 1.9 points, s 0.5, noise dominates. Island-wide: m 212, s 545. Also shown, not ranked: skill against persistence, median absolute error, 50%/90% coverage.
