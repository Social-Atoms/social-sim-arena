## Target

- **Population** — Rents paid by tenants (contract rent, not asking rent) in BLS's CPI housing sample, US city average, all urban consumers (CPI-U), seasonally adjusted by BLS. Each sampled unit is re-priced every six months, so a month's change mixes the six-month changes of one panel [owner doc; BLS methodology, not re-read]. National only. That is a weakness under the track's 'geography where available' aim, not a disqualification (lesson 14). BLS also publishes not-seasonally-adjusted rent indexes every month for the four census regions and for metro areas [verified: CUUR0100/0200/0300/0400SEHA and 15 metro series such as CUURS12ASEHA (New York) and CUURS37ASEHA (Dallas) carry monthly points through 2026M09]. A ten-metro profile of them is noted as future work (owner decision, rev 2: national at launch).
- **Window** — Reference month M (the CPI collection month). It is released in the CPI news release of month M+1, usually between the 10th and the 15th, at 08:30 ET.
- **Timezone** — America/New_York
- **Calculation** — 100 x (L_M / L_{M-1} - 1), where L_M and L_{M-1} are the CUSR0000SEHA index levels for M and M-1 (3 decimals), both taken from the same release-day response. Not rounded before scoring. Taking both levels from one response matters every February, when BLS revises five years of seasonally adjusted levels: the M-1 level then differs from the one known at lock, and a change built across two vintages would mix two seasonal factors. Example: September 2026 = 100 x (450.429 / 449.321 - 1) = 0.2466 [verified], which the press table prints as 0.2.
- **Unit** — Percent, one-month change, seasonally adjusted

## Lock, scoring and resolution

- **Lock** — Release minus 48 hours: 08:30 America/New_York two days before the BLS release date. Owner update 2026-11-10: no unscored trial requirement. Every admitted future round is scored, subject to the existing reference, capture and cancellation rules. First proposed scored lock: 2026-12-08T13:30:00Z. Publish on main before lock; a missing or invalid reference does not become a fabricated baseline.
- **Baseline** — Ranked zero point (owner decision, rev 2): mean_k with k = 6 and an rms_n spread with n = 12 (one parameterised reference family, owner decision G1). The mean is the six most recent available one-month changes in the lock-time response; sd is the root-mean-square error of that same rule over the 12 most recent target months before M (G2). All of it is computed from the at-lock vintage only. Months without a change (October and November 2025, since there is no October 2025 level) are skipped, not filled. Measured on 36 target months 2023-07 to 2026-09 [verified computation on the 2026-11-09 API response, modern backfill: it uses today's seasonal factors, not the ones in force at each past lock]: mean6 MAE 0.061, mean CRPS 0.0466; an entrant who submits mean6 and only rescales its sd gains at most +0.8% (k 0.80); 90% interval coverage 0.94; sd 0.045 to 0.133. Persistence (last month's change) with its own RMS-12 sd: MAE 0.072, CRPS 0.0545, best rescale gain +0.1%, coverage 0.86. So mean6 beats persistence by 14.5% in CRPS. With persistence as the zero point, an entrant submitting the 6-month average would collect about +14% skill for free. With the arena's default persistence spread (sd 1.5) as the zero point, submitting persistence itself with an honest spread scores +84.6% (CRPS 0.0545 vs 0.3532), the lesson-12 trap. Also shown, not ranked: persistence (mean_k, k = 1, with an rms_12 spread) and 50%/90% coverage. Zillow's CPI forecast is not used (owner decision, rev 2). Shocks: April 2026 printed +0.545% against mean6 0.224 and persistence 0.190, a miss of about 6 sd. BLS had warned April would be atypical because of the October 2025 gap in the six-month panel [owner doc]. The yearly mean change fell from 0.67 (2022) to 0.26 (2026 to date) [verified], so a long-window mean would be biased upward.
- **Resolution** — The first archived API response captured at or after the release time (08:30 ET on the scheduled day) whose newest CUSR0000SEHA period is M. The value is computed from L_M and L_{M-1} in that same response. Later revisions, including the February seasonal re-estimation, never change a resolved round. The archived body (bytes, sha256, request JSON, fetched_at) is the record.
- **Missing data policy** — (1) Release rescheduled before the lock: the lock moves to the new release minus 48h. Rescheduled after the lock: forecasts stand, and the round resolves on the delayed release if it comes within 45 days of the original date; otherwise it is cancelled. (2) Release cancelled, as October 2025 was during the 2025 lapse in appropriations (API footnote code X: 'Data unavailable due to the 2025 lapse in appropriations') [verified]: the round is cancelled. (3) L_{M-1} missing (M-1 was a cancelled month): no one-month change exists, and the round is cancelled, not built from a two-month change. (4) Lock reference: if no capture from the 24 hours before the lock carries M-1, the round refuses to post its reference (lesson 13). (5) A response with status other than REQUEST_SUCCEEDED, or a value '-' for month M, is not a resolution capture; the next capture within the window is used.
- **Scoring** — SSA topline scoring for a number: {mean, sd} with sd > 0, or a quantile map including 0.5, scored with CRPS (arena target_type continuous_normal). Skill is 1 - CRPS(entrant) / CRPS(zero point), where the zero point is the round's zero_point reference: mean_k (k = 6) with an rms_n spread (n = 12). Implementation adds one parameterised reference, e.g. zero_point {"rule": "mean_k", "k": 6, "spread": "rms_n", "n": 12}, through the same baselines.zero_point mechanism as 'yoy4' on branch track/seattle-community-calls-v2, not a new name per card (G1). Its mean and sd are published and hashed at lock. Room for skill [verified]: with the honest-spread reference, mean CRPS is about 0.047 points. The arena noise floor is not meaningful for a CPI component (it is not a sample survey in the arena's sense; maintainers confirm, docs/sources.md §1.4).
