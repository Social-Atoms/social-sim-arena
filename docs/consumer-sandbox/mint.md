# U.S. Mint Weekly-Increment Sandbox

Target: net unit sales for one fixed-supply SKU in the week ending Sunday
October 18, 2026. Lock before the week begins (illustrative October 11 at
23:00 ET), then use two manually downloaded, publisher-valid Sunday cumulative
reports. Do not substitute a later corrected report for the pinned first print.

Example: a cumulative 100 units on October 11 and 90 on October 18 resolve to
-10 units for the week. A Normal(mean=0, sd=15) forecast is scored with the
arena's existing CRPS. Negative corrections are legitimate. This synthetic
fixture is not a selected Mint product or a live round; a formal target still
needs verified fixed-supply, buyer, batch, release-time and rights conditions.
