# Netflix Top 10 Adapter

The parser validates the official country TSV, filters `US`, preserves film and
season identity, and requires complete ranks 1-10. A prospective design would
lock before Monday's measurement week and define new-title candidates before
the lock. Netflix Terms of Use prohibit automated access and machine-learning
benchmarking, so this adapter is retained for authorized/manual evidence only
and cannot activate a benchmark without written authorization.
