# ACSI Industry Adapter

The adapter accepts explicitly quoted release prose and benchmark tables. It
keeps industry, segment, company and brand scopes distinct, binds each score to
its study year and dated release, and refuses undated cells. A proposed round
locks one week before an expected Tuesday release only when a forward calendar
or approved exception exists. ACSI remains permission-needed because complete
terms and redistribution rights are not established; no target activates.
