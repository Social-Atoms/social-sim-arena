Mint yields 1,396 cumulative observations and 698 weekly net changes: 131
positive, 555 zero and 12 negative. Negative adjustments are retained. Different
SKU bundles are not summed as individual coins. The 698 cumulative histories
are diagnostics, not additional approved forecasting targets.

Mean new top-ten items per consecutive week: Netflix films 5.1703, Netflix TV
4.2826, Steam 3.9608 and NYT ISBN editions 4.0980. These are retrospective
diagnostics, not leakage-free backtests or evidence of forecasting skill.

The local prototype passed all 33 consumer tests, replayed 125 retained vintages
and validated the review schema and exact preservation of original site records.
Its appended copy contains 2,177 scalar histories and five ranking histories,
including the existing 78 scalar histories and one ranking history. New data
accounts for 6,804 scalar observations and 658 weekly lists. Counts include
diagnostic totals, sparse annual instruments and aggregates.

These tests were run on the separate implementation branch, not on this
documentation-only branch. Follow-up source PRs must expose the relevant code
and fixtures for independent review. The permission-cleared Michigan, SCE and
Mint artifacts are attached to the review snapshot release linked from the
index. Other source prototypes remain local pending source-specific rights.

Historical downloads retain their actual capture times. They do not establish
the original first print or justify a formal forecasting lock. No paid forecast
call, active-task change or production site-data change was made during collection.
