# ACSI Industry Studies

**What the number is.** Company-level customer satisfaction, ACSI score 0-100,
within a named industry/study year. A company's true cadence is usually annual,
even though different industries publish across the year. Respondents are
customers in the study's sampling window, not a weekly general population panel.

**Where it is published.** Official index:
`https://theacsi.com/news-and-resources/press-releases/`.
The initial index exposed 100 pages. The official sitemaps identified six more
from January-March 2021; all 106 listed 2021-2026 release pages are now captured.
They include industry, national, and other announcements. Public industry pages
also contain brand-score tables: 47 pages are archived and 46 have explicitly
selected national tables. These captures do not prove original first prints.

**How the arena reads it.** No code on `dev`. The prototype corpus collector,
benchmark-table scraper (`tools/` script) and transcription importer were merged
to `dev` and then removed pending source terms; this card is an evaluation
record only, and the description below is of that removed code and its local
results. A local corpus manifest records URL, original HTML,
hash, fetch time, and status. The original four-brand sample is extended by
explicit scores in automobile, travel, restaurant, electronics, finance,
insurance, retail and telecommunications release prose. A table importer adds
1,084 verified score cells. Tables
are pinned by population and position; each score is checked against the exact
year column, company cell, row, and dated study release. The generated catalog
distinguishes company/brand instruments from industry/segment/other-company aggregates.
Current benchmark prior-year columns are explicitly labeled current vintages.
Another 82 cells lack a unique dated release and stay outside scalar histories.

**How a question resolves.** Pending review. A new task needs either an official
forward announcement or an approved calendar exception. A one-week-early lock
is a proposal, not approval. Keep company/industry/year identity and resolve
against the exact initially captured release after its public appearance.
Historical pages captured today cannot establish that they were never revised.
The scalar date is the associated study-release date, not proof of first public
availability. Some benchmark-page publication metadata precedes the study date;
release-calendar review must examine that before any forecasting lock is set.

**What to watch.** Promotional use requires ACSI consent; that restriction
neither automatically prohibits all research nor authorizes publication of raw
bodies. All corpus/score data stays private. No source approval was granted.
Many instruments have only one or two annual observations; release-prose
extraction extends selected histories back to 2021. Movement cannot establish weekly task
availability. Regional, device/fuel-type, experience-benchmark, and stale retail
segment tables are excluded. The old search-engines URL now contains sports
betting data, so its actual table defines the population. Priority remains high,
but exhaustive first-print history, permissions,
entity mapping, forward dates, and primary/backup ownership remain open.
Specific customer eligibility matters: USAA is restricted to military-affiliated
customers, and hotel groups, hotel sub-brands, and product categories are distinct
instruments. The local coverage report records unresolved publisher labels.
