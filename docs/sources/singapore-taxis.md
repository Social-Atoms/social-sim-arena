# Singapore free taxis in Downtown Core, Friday 23:00 (LTA, via data.gov.sg)

> **Owner update, 2026-11-10:** revision 3 removes unscored trials. All admitted future rounds are scored, starting with `sg-taxi-dtc-2026-12-11` (lock `2026-12-09T15:00:00Z`). The older trial/start-date discussion below is historical; current rules and byte hashes are in the v3 card and rounds file. Capture validity, references and cancellation rules remain mandatory.


**What the number is.** How many taxis available for hire LTA's feed places inside the Downtown Core planning area at Friday 23:00 Singapore time. Free taxis are those not hired and not busy, so a lower number means higher demand (and drivers going off shift also lower it). SSA defines and resolves this metric; no publisher releases it, and every round says so. The rules are the approved Data Card [`sg-taxi-downtown-fri2300@v3`](../data-cards/sg-taxi-downtown-fri2300.json) (sha256 `0b03bf5c55e44dbcec3537ad155c86b767544c1d9d70a865697a4332d02d6c43`). Over the 26 valid Fridays to 2026-11-06 the count averaged 162.5.

**Where it is published.** data.gov.sg Taxi Availability (`d_e25662f1a062dd046453926aa284ba64`), keyless: `api.data.gov.sg/v1/transport/taxi-availability?date_time=<SGT minute>` returns a GeoJSON MultiPoint of free taxis for any past minute. The boundary is URA's Master Plan 2019 Planning Area Boundary (No Sea) (`d_4765db0e87b9c86336792efe8a1f7a66`), committed once under `sources/sg-ref/ura-mp2019-planning-area-nosea/2026-11-09.geojson` and pinned for the season by sha256 `f23856251b467089f788d0fff72ef5a38e753f21aa69b4352401d7ed50d380cc` (the code refuses other bytes). Licence: *Contains information from Taxi Availability and Master Plan 2019 Planning Area Boundary (No Sea) accessed on the date in each file's path from data.gov.sg which is made available under the terms of the [Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).* No LTA or URA endorsement is implied.

**How the arena reads it.** [`ssa/adapters/sg_taxi.py`](../../ssa/adapters/sg_taxi.py), from its own workflow, [`sg-timed-capture.yml`](../../.github/workflows/sg-timed-capture.yml): Friday 23:30 SGT, one retry Saturday 01:00 SGT. It requests 23:00 and, only if that snapshot is invalid, 23:01, 23:02, 23:05 and 23:10 in turn, filing every body exactly as served under `sources/lta-rt/taxi/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<HHMM>.json` (keyed by the minute requested) with its fetch time, URL, sha256 and size in `sources/lta-rt/taxi/fetches.json`; a held minute costs no request. A run also fills up to 12 missing past Fridays of the last 36, labelled `backfill` (fetched after the fact from the past-minute endpoint, card policy (5)), so the reference has its history from the first pilot.

**How a question resolves.** Metric definition v1: a snapshot is valid if its timestamp is at most 120 s before the minute and its island count is at least 30% of the median of the last 4 valid Fridays in the archive; the first valid minute in order is counted, points inside DOWNTOWN CORE by ray casting, holes excluded, first polygon in file order. No valid snapshot among the five minutes cancels the round (on the card's data: 3 of 66 Fridays, 3 of the last 17). Forecasts lock Wednesday 23:00 SGT. The ranked reference `mean4_rms13` is the mean of the last 4 valid Fridays in the archive as it stood at lock (fewer than 4 valid among the last 6: not posted), sd the root-mean-square of the same rule's errors over the 13 most recent valid target Fridays; persistence with its own spread is shown, unranked. Replayed on the card's 66 snapshots, the adapter reproduces the card: 63 valid, the three glitch Fridays cancelled, MAE 44.8, CRPS 34.0, 90% coverage 0.88.

**What to watch.** Two readings the card leaves open and the code fixes, for maintainers to confirm: a snapshot whose point list does not match `taxi_count`, or that does not parse, is invalid ("points missing", policy (4)); while the archive holds fewer than 4 earlier valid Fridays the median uses those it has. A minute that was never archived keeps the round pending until Saturday 12:00 SGT, then cancels it. *Regime change*: island counts ran 2,589 to 5,176 from October 2025 to mid-January 2026, unexplained. *Datacenter access is untested*: a 403 or 429 on GitHub runners means a local courier.
