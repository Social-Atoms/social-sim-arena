# Singapore COE Category A premium (LTA, via data.gov.sg)

**What the number is.** The Category A Certificate of Entitlement quota premium, in Singapore dollars: the clearing price every successful bidder pays at one LTA open bidding exercise (two a month). Category A is non-fully-electric cars up to 1,600 cc and 97 kW, and fully electric cars up to 110 kW, as defined from the May 2022 (1) exercise. The rules are the approved Data Card [`sg-coe-cat-a-premium@v2`](../data-cards/sg-coe-cat-a-premium.json) (sha256 `54eb7de69bb6c10b0f465dd0d9314a55cc514009d82d1ee2c932a8fc4cf9e0e6`). Over the 52 exercises to 2026-11 (1) the premium averaged S$109,856; the last eight were 126,000 to 139,001.

**Where it is published.** data.gov.sg dataset [`d_69b3380ad7e51aff3a7dcc84eba52b8a`](https://data.gov.sg/datasets?query=COE&resultId=d_69b3380ad7e51aff3a7dcc84eba52b8a) ("COE Bidding Results / Prices", managed by the Land Transport Authority), keyless through the datastore API, about 1.5 hours after an exercise closes (98 minutes for 2026-11 (1)). The calendar is LTA's published [2026 schedule](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/dam/onemotoring/Buying/PDF/COE/COE_Bidding_Schedule_for_Year_2026.pdf): Monday 12:00 to Wednesday 16:00 SGT, except February (2) (to Friday) and June (1) (Tuesday to Thursday). The 2027 schedule was not posted on 2026-11-09. Licence: *Contains information from COE Bidding Results / Prices accessed on the date in each capture's name from data.gov.sg which is made available under the terms of the [Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).* Nothing here suggests LTA endorses SSA.

**How the arena reads it.** [`ssa/adapters/sg_coe.py`](../../ssa/adapters/sg_coe.py), from the 6-hourly refresh (`refresh.yml`, non-blocking). A run costs no request unless a rule needs a capture: once in the 12 hours before a lock, and every run from close + 2 h until a capture holds the exercise's Category A row (at most to close + 48 h). A capture is the metadata body plus every datastore page, filed exactly as served under `sources/coe/<YYYY-MM-DDTHHMM>Z/`, each hashed into `sources/coe/fetches.json`; pages that disagree on `total` or a short capture are refused. Counts are text and lose their thousands commas before casting. The exercise schedule is `sg_coe.SCHEDULE`, copied from the PDF; an exercise not on it has no lock and no round.

**How a question resolves.** Forecasts lock one hour before bidding opens (normally Monday 11:00 SGT), because the running price is public once bidding starts. The ranked reference `mean1_rms26` (baselines `mean_k`, k = 1, on an exercise index) is the previous exercise's premium from the capture archived at lock (no more than 24 hours old), with sd the root-mean-square of the last 26 exercise-to-exercise changes in that capture; 27 consecutive exercises are needed, or the round is not posted. The mean of the last two exercises is shown, unranked. The round resolves on the first capture at or after close + 2 h whose rows hold the exercise's Category A premium (above 0); none by close + 48 h cancels it, and a later change to the row never changes a resolved round. On the 48 exercises to 2026-11 (1) the reference scored mean CRPS S$2,713 with 94% 90%-coverage (reproduced in `tests/test_sg_coe.py`).

**What to watch.** *The lock is 53 hours before the close, not 48*: the season validator's default source contract needs a rule for `sg_coe` before the proposed rounds ([`sg-coe-cat-a-premium.rounds.json`](../data-cards/sg-coe-cat-a-premium.rounds.json)) can pass it. *The quota* is announced by LTA before bidding opens and goes into the question text at posting. *2027* waits for LTA's schedule: add it to `SCHEDULE` first. *Category change*: LTA is consulting on a new car categorisation; a change between posting and close cancels the round. *Datacenter access is untested*: a 403 or 429 on the refresh runners means a local courier.
