# Morning Consult

**What the number is.** The share of US registered voters who approve of the President's job performance, from Morning Consult's continuously fielded online survey, roughly 2,200 registered voters per wave. Percent; the registered-voter base reads several points different from the same survey's adult base (in June 2025 one wave read 49.8 among adults and 46.2 among registered voters), so the population is part of the definition.

**Where it is published.** Weekly, on Morning Consult's [tracker page](https://pro.morningconsult.com/trackers/donald-trump-congress-policy-republicans-polling).

**How the arena reads it.** From the Silver Bulletin poll database (see [silver-bulletin.md](silver-bulletin.md)), filtered to pollster Morning Consult, subgroup "All polls", population RV; the raw marginals. Declared in [`ssa/series.py`](https://github.com/Social-Atoms/social-sim-arena/blob/main/ssa/series.py), read by [`ssa/adapters/silverbulletin.py`](https://github.com/Social-Atoms/social-sim-arena/blob/main/ssa/adapters/silverbulletin.py).

**How a question resolves.** On the first Morning Consult wave to enter the archived sheet after the question froze.

**What to watch.** Publication times are knowable but the source has not been stable enough to schedule against; issue #115 records the state of that.
