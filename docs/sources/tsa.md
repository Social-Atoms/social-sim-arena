# TSA checkpoint travel numbers (tsa.gov)

**What the number is.** How many travelers TSA screens at its checkpoints nationwide in one Monday-to-Sunday week: the sum of seven daily numbers from TSA's passenger-volumes page. Over the 52 weeks to 2026-10-26 a week averaged 17.3 million. Whether crew are included is not stated. The rules are the approved Data Card [`tsa-checkpoint-weekly@v2`](../data-cards/tsa-checkpoint-weekly.json) (sha256 `5c69cafebad1610bb2c8aad0d58f706d28ad8b7cf623828debb916133d01c9f8`); the card's pilot and first scored rounds are proposed in [`tsa-checkpoint-weekly.rounds.json`](../data-cards/tsa-checkpoint-weekly.rounds.json), not in the season.

**Where it is published.** [tsa.gov/travel/passenger-volumes](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/passenger-volumes) for the current year and `/travel/passenger-volumes/<YYYY>` for earlier years: one table, `Date | Numbers`, updated "Monday through Friday by 9 a.m." ET, so Friday to Sunday post on Monday and nothing changes over the weekend. There is no release calendar, so SSA sets and resolves the metric (docs/sources.md §1.1). TSA's site notice says the information "is considered public information and may be distributed or copied", and robots.txt allows `/travel/`. Inventory rights stay `unresolved` until maintainers decide; the card proposes `approved`.

**How the arena reads it.** [`ssa/adapters/tsa_volumes.py`](../../ssa/adapters/tsa_volumes.py), from a non-blocking step in the 6-hourly refresh: once per UTC day, the current page and then the two previous year pages, 10 s apart, with the arena's honest User-Agent. All three are parsed before anything is written, then filed exactly as served under `sources/tsa_volumes/<fetch day, UTC>/{current,<YYYY>,<YYYY>}.html`, write-once, with each page's URL, sha256, size, row count and newest date in `sources/tsa_volumes/fetches.json`. A page with no table, another header, or fewer rows than the previous capture of the same year is a failed capture: nothing is filed and the failure is appended to `sources/tsa_volumes/failures.json`. A date on two pages with different numbers is logged and read from the current page.

**If the runners are refused** (403, 429, or a challenge page instead of the table): never a browser User-Agent, a headless browser or any other workaround (the owner's sketch suggested them; lessons 3 and 11). First fallback, a **local courier**: a maintainer runs the same command, `python -m ssa.adapters.tsa_volumes capture`, from their own Mac once a day (in the courier set-up that carries Civiqs), and commits `sources/tsa_volumes`. Second fallback, **by hand**: a named person saves the three pages (browser "save page as, HTML only", or one plain `curl -A 'social-simulation-arena/1.0 (research benchmark; contact via repository)'`) on Saturday for the lock and on Tuesday after 12:00 ET for the resolution; a manual ingest for this route is not written yet (open item), so the courier is the fallback that runs today.

**How a question resolves.** Forecasts lock Sunday 23:59 ET before the week, from the newest capture taken between Saturday 00:00 ET and the lock (none and the round is not posted). The ranked reference, `yoy_d35_d4`, is one setting of the `yoy_k` family: R = the days from D-35 to the newest date posted, at most Thursday D-4, divided by the same days 364 days earlier, times last year's week D-364; its spread is the root-mean-square error of the same rule (each past week with its normal end W-4) over the 13 newest target weeks complete in the capture. Any missing date inside the range, or in its year-earlier days or last year's week, means the round is not posted. Persistence on the week D-14 is shown, unranked. The week resolves on the first capture at or after Tuesday D+8 12:00 ET that holds all seven dates; none by Friday D+11 12:00 ET and it is cancelled. Later TSA revisions never change a resolved round.

**What to watch.** *Revisions and holiday delays are measured by the pilot*: two unscored rounds (locks 11-29 and 12-06), the Thanksgiving week's completeness on 12-01, and week totals compared across captures (`tsa_volumes.pilot_change`, under 0.2%) decide, on 2026-12-18, whether the 2027-01-03 round is scored. *A shock in last year's week returns a year later*: the reference multiplies by last year's week, so the November 2025 flight-reduction week makes the 2026-11-09 reference too low. *January*: the current page restarts at 1/1 and the old year moves to its own page; the capture asks for the two years before the newest date on the current page. *Holidays*: Christmas and New Year's Day fall on other weekdays than in the comparison weeks and are flagged in the round text (`ssa/us_holidays.py`). *Datacenter access is untested*; the first refresh run is the test.
