# Seattle police calls for service (City of Seattle)

**What the number is.** How many calls Seattle residents made to the police in one Monday-to-Sunday week, Seattle local time: distinct dispatch (CAD) events that reached the Seattle Police Department by 911, non-emergency telephone, text message or in-person complaint. Officer-initiated activity (`ONVIEW`, `PROACTIVE (OFFICER INITIATED)`), automated alarm-company calls and administrative entries are not counted. The rules are the approved Data Card [`seattle-community-calls@v3`](../data-cards/seattle-community-calls.json) (sha256 `843ae9ba7b33131dfe674d520a41ea402cc655e34c9986b108875348a1654ce3`), the first card of the Urban Track. Over the 52 weeks to 2026-10-26 the weekly count averaged about 4,750, from 3,698 (the week of Christmas 2025) to 5,285.

**Where it is published.** The [Call Data](https://cos-data.seattle.gov/Public-Safety/Call-Data/33kz-ixgy/about_data) dataset (`33kz-ixgy`) on the City of Seattle Open Data Portal, refreshed daily, keyless through the Socrata SODA API. There is **no release calendar**: rows are posted daily and trail the clock by about three days, in whole days (on 2026-11-08 22:10Z the newest queue time was 2026-11-05 23:59:09). So SSA both sets this metric and resolves it (docs/sources.md §1.1), and the definition is the committed card. The dataset is public domain (`licenseId PUBLIC_DOMAIN`), and the City's [Open Data Policy OD-1](https://www.seattle.gov/documents/Departments/Tech/OpenData/COS_OpenDataPolicy.pdf) §D and §E publish portal data without restrictions on use, copying or redistribution, asking only that the source and version be identified: *Contains City of Seattle / Seattle Police Department Call Data (33kz-ixgy), as archived by the arena on the date in each file name, unmodified.*

**How the arena reads it.** [`ssa/adapters/seattle_cad.py`](../../ssa/adapters/seattle_cad.py) sends one grouped request a day, `count(distinct cad_event_number)` by queue day and `call_type` for the trailing 630 days, and files the body exactly as served under `sources/seattle_cad/<fetch day, UTC>.json`, write-once per day: a second run the same day costs no request. Each capture's fetch time, URL, sha256 and size are also kept in `sources/seattle_cad/fetches.json`, because the lock and resolution rules are stated in instants and the provenance manifest keeps only the newest fetch. The parser raises on an empty or truncated body, a repeated (day, call type) pair or a non-integer count; a call type on neither list is excluded and reported.

**How a question resolves.** Forecasts lock Sunday 23:59 PT before the week. The ranked reference (`zero_point` `yoy4`) is fixed at lock from the capture archived then (no more than 24 hours old): the 4-week year-over-year ratio of sums over the four settled weeks starting D-35 to D-14, never the week that ends at the lock, times last year's 364-day-aligned week, scored as a normal whose sd is the root-mean-square error of the same rule over the 13 most recent target weeks complete in that capture. Persistence on the week D-14 is shown, unranked. The week resolves on the first capture at or after Tuesday 12:00 PT eight days after the week ends; if a target day is missing, zero or below 40% of its 28-day median, the next capture until Friday 12:00 PT is used, and after that the round is cancelled, never resolved from a later pull.

**What to watch.** *How late calls keep arriving is not measured yet*: a two-week unscored pilot of daily captures from 2026-11-30 decides, on 2026-12-11, whether the eight-day wait is enough (`seattle_cad.pilot_growth`, growth under 0.5% after the +7-day capture). *The data is a backfill*: SPD runs a full refresh about twice a year, and earlier versions of the dataset withheld about 40% of calls, so history read today is not what was first posted. *Holidays are the hard weeks*: Thanksgiving and Christmas run 15 to 22% below an ordinary week; the 364-day shift aligns Thanksgiving, MLK, Presidents', Memorial and Labor Day, not Christmas or New Year's Day. *Datacenter access is untested*: a 403 or 429 on the refresh runners means the capture moves to a local courier.
