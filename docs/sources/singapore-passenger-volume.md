# Singapore passenger volume: Jurong East MRT and Boon Lay bus interchange (LTA DataMall)

> **Start archiving the day the key exists.** DataMall serves only the last three months of Passenger Volume files. Every month not downloaded within three months is lost for good, and no round can use it. On 2026-11-08 nothing is archived: if the key arrives after 2026-12-10, August 2026 is gone; after 2027-01-10, October too; the January 2027 pilot's reference month (November 2026) must be archived by 2027-02-10.

**What the number is.** For one calendar month, the average daily fare-card tap-ins on weekdays that are not public holidays: at Jurong East MRT (`EW24/NS1`, card [`sg-lta-pv-train-jurong-east@v2`](../data-cards/sg-lta-pv-train-jurong-east.json), sha256 `643a964dace5d09507d8660c97d921636f5110a0806f568ada10951704f5003e`), and bus boardings at Boon Lay Interchange (stop `22009`, card [`sg-lta-pv-bus-boon-lay-int@v2`](../data-cards/sg-lta-pv-bus-boon-lay-int.json), sha256 `67073fb81a518a661b19fd9c0ac17e6f937332f6c664f3b9190632abaa84ba9f`). The sum of `TOTAL_TAP_IN_VOLUME` over the month's WEEKDAY rows for the code, over the Monday-to-Friday dates that are not gazetted public holidays (MOM's list, frozen in the round), rounded to a whole tap.

**Where it is published.** LTA DataMall `PV/Train` and `PV/Bus` (API User Guide v6.11, sections 2.5 and 2.8): with an `AccountKey` header, `GET https://datamall2.mytransport.sg/ltaodataservice/PV/Train?Date=YYYYMM` returns `value[0].Link`, a short-lived S3 link to a zipped CSV (`YEAR_MONTH, DAY_TYPE, TIME_PER_HOUR, PT_TYPE, PT_CODE, TOTAL_TAP_IN_VOLUME, TOTAL_TAP_OUT_VOLUME`, Annex A), generated "by 10th of every month" for the previous month. Rights: DataMall's API Terms of Service and the Singapore Open Data Licence (inventory row `sg_lta_pv`, verdict proposed for maintainers), with one website clause of unclear scope the cards record. Licence notice once data is published: *Contains information from LTA DataMall Passenger Volume by Train Stations / Bus Stops, made available under the terms of the [Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).*

**Registration (a maintainer, once; nothing runs until then).** As the cards give it:

1. Open <https://datamall.lta.gov.sg/content/datamall/en/request-for-api.html> in a browser.
2. Fill Name, Email (a project mailbox), Contact No, Company Name `Social Simulation Arena (MIT research benchmark)`; tick Purpose `Research`.
3. Description, verbatim: *Public research benchmark (Social Simulation Arena). We download the monthly Passenger Volume files (PV/Train, PV/Bus) once a month, archive them unchanged, and publish derived station and stop figures with the Singapore Open Data Licence attribution.*
4. Tick *I agree to and accept the terms stated herein the Singapore Open Data Licence, and Terms of Service for API and SDK*; leave the mailing list unticked; submit.
5. Store the emailed key only as the repository secret `LTA_API_KEY` (API ToS cl. 10(b): keep credentials confidential). Never commit it.
6. The same day, archive what DataMall still serves: `LTA_API_KEY=... python -m ssa.adapters.sg_lta_pv capture train` and `... capture bus` (each archives the three available months it lacks), and commit `sources/lta-pv/`.
7. Then wire the same two commands into `refresh.yml` as a non-blocking step with `LTA_API_KEY: ${{ secrets.LTA_API_KEY }}` (Route A), or, if runners are blocked or maintainers prefer the key off CI, run them by hand on the 10th or 11th (Route B: `python -m ssa.adapters.sg_lta_pv ingest train 2026-10 file.zip` files a zip downloaded by hand).

Optional, only if a maintainer decides to send it (nobody on our side does): the cards draft a question to LTA via the DataMall Contact Us page about the website Terms of Use clauses 5, 21 and 23 and about files older than three months.

**How the arena reads it.** [`ssa/adapters/sg_lta_pv.py`](../../ssa/adapters/sg_lta_pv.py), **not wired into any workflow**. It refuses to run without `LTA_API_KEY`, sends the key only as a header to DataMall (never to S3, never into a file), downloads the zip at once and files it as `sources/lta-pv/<train|bus>/<YYYYMM>.zip` with the URL stripped of its signature, fetch time, sha256 and size in `fetches.json`. A file for another month, of another `PT_TYPE`, with too few codes (train 150, bus 4,500), or different bytes for a month already held is refused; codes stay text; rows with an empty hour are dropped and counted. Tests use fixtures shaped like the documented response (`tests/test_sg_lta_pv.py`).

**How a question resolves.** Forecasts lock 23:59 SGT the day before the target month. The ranked reference is baselines `mean_k` (k = 1, lag = 1): the newest month archived at lock, two before the target (missing: not posted). Its spread is interim, sd = mean x 5.43% (train) or 6.66% (bus), LTA's system-wide two-month change 2023-2025, until 12 resolved months are archived; then the root-mean-square of the rule's own relative errors over the last 12. A round resolves on the first file for the month archived at or after 00:00 SGT on the 10th of the next month; none by 23:59 on the 17th, the code missing, or zero weekday tap-ins in any hour 06 to 22 cancels it.

**What to watch.** *The station code*: the guide's own sample writes a merged interchange as `EW14-NS26` (hyphen), while the card names `EW24/NS1`. The code matches the card exactly and has an empty alias table, so a file that says `EW24-NS1` cancels the round; check on the first download and revise the card or announce the alias before any lock. *The release hour* on the 10th is not stated; the pilots log it. Proposed rounds: [`sg-lta-pv-train-jurong-east.rounds.json`](../data-cards/sg-lta-pv-train-jurong-east.rounds.json), [`sg-lta-pv-bus-boon-lay-int.rounds.json`](../data-cards/sg-lta-pv-bus-boon-lay-int.rounds.json) (January and February 2027 pilots, first scored March 2027); their ~41-day lock-to-release gap needs a validator contract for `sg_lta_pv`.
