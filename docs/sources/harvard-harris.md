# Harvard CAPS / Harris Poll

**What the number is.** The share of respondents who strongly or somewhat approve of the President's job performance, from the monthly Harvard CAPS / Harris Poll. The survey filters on registered voters and weights to the US adult population; the scored number is the approve net among those respondents as published. Percent.

**Where it is published.** [harvardharrispoll.com](https://harvardharrispoll.com/), one page per poll with a topline, key results and crosstabs as PDFs. Roughly monthly, with no release calendar: months are skipped without notice (no June 2026 poll), and neither the page slugs nor the PDF names follow a pattern.

**How the arena reads it.** The archive is the source of truth. Every six-hour refresh watches the publisher's all-polls index, follows only Topline document pages newer than the committed catalog cursor, and archives a novel PDF only after strict parsing. `pdftotext` converts the document; the parser finds the exact presidential-approval table headed by `M3` in the separately verified 2017-2020 releases or `M3ALT` in the current term. Files are write-once under `sources/hhpoll/`, named by the document's own production stamp rather than the download day. Every number is cross-checked before it leaves: the published nets must sum to about 100, and each must agree with its own count over the weighted base. A broken index, moved PDF, or changed table degrades the source visibly and serves the last validated archive; it never files an unparsed document. The reader and watcher are both in [`ssa/adapters/hhpoll.py`](https://github.com/Social-Atoms/social-sim-arena/blob/main/ssa/adapters/hhpoll.py).

**How a question resolves.** On the first topline archived with a production stamp after the lock, so every question on this task carries `release_estimated: true` and resolves on the next published wave.

**What to watch.** The committed archive holds 62 validated waves: 46 from the first term (February 2017 through November 2020) and 16 from the current term (February 2025 through July 2026). There are no presidential-approval waves during the intervening Biden administration, and skipped months remain gaps rather than interpolated observations. The catalog records each publisher page and PDF URL. A new page that changes the question wording or table layout is a source failure requiring review, not permission to weaken the parser.
