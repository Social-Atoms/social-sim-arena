# Singapore HDB carpark occupancy in Clementi, Saturday 20:00 (HDB, via data.gov.sg)

> **Owner update, 2026-11-10:** revision 3 removes unscored trials. All admitted future rounds are scored, starting with `sg-hdb-clementi-2026-12-12` (lock `2026-12-10T12:00:00Z`). The older trial/start-date discussion below is historical; current rules and byte hashes are in the v3 card and rounds file. Capture validity, references and cancellation rules remain mandatory.


**What the number is.** The share of car lots taken, in percent, across the HDB (public-housing) carparks of the Clementi planning area at Saturday 20:00 Singapore time: total lots minus available lots, summed over carparks, over total lots. SSA defines and resolves this metric; no publisher releases it. The rules are the approved Data Card [`sg-hdb-carpark-clementi-sat2000@v3`](../data-cards/sg-hdb-carpark-clementi-sat2000.json) (sha256 `7af26df3290884a52743959da7cf28a2e94fa8cd9ec4495c8acdcb8c9997ade3`). The 66 Saturdays measured for the card ranged from 36.9% to 49.0%; it moves about a point a week, close to its noise, so expect small skills.

**Where it is published.** data.gov.sg Carpark Availability (`d_ca933a644e55d34fe21f28b8052fac63`), keyless: `api.data.gov.sg/v1/transport/carpark-availability?date_time=<SGT minute>` serves any past minute. Carparks are placed with HDB Carpark Information (`d_23f946fa557947f93a8043bbef41dd09`, SVY21 coordinates converted to WGS84) and URA's Master Plan 2019 planning-area boundary, both committed once under `sources/sg-ref/` and pinned by sha256 (`ssa/adapters/sg_common.py`): 48 HDB carparks fall in Clementi, 16 multi-storey and 32 surface. Licence: *Contains information from Carpark Availability, HDB Carpark Information and Master Plan 2019 Planning Area Boundary (No Sea) accessed on the date in each file's path from data.gov.sg which is made available under the terms of the [Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).* No HDB, URA or LTA endorsement is implied. Mall carparks (DataMall, key required) are not in this card.

**How the arena reads it.** [`ssa/adapters/sg_carpark.py`](../../ssa/adapters/sg_carpark.py), from [`sg-timed-capture.yml`](../../.github/workflows/sg-timed-capture.yml): Saturday 20:30 SGT, one retry at 22:00 SGT. It requests 20:00 and, only if invalid, 20:01, 20:02, 20:05 and 20:10, filing every body as served under `sources/lta-rt/carpark-hdb/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<HHMM>.json` with `fetches.json` beside it, and fills up to 12 missing past Saturdays of the last 30 per run, labelled `backfill`. A body is about 320 KB, so the archive grows about 17 MB a year.

**How a question resolves.** Metric definition v1: valid if `items` is non-empty, the timestamp is at most 120 s before the minute and the body has at least 1,900 car rows; keep car rows with total lots above 0 updated within 60 minutes of the snapshot, over the 48 pinned Clementi carparks. Fewer than 36 of them passing cancels the round, as does no valid snapshot among the five minutes; a row with more available than total lots is dropped and listed, a feed carpark missing from the pinned table is excluded and counted. Forecasts lock Thursday 20:00 SGT. The ranked reference `mean1_rms13` is last Saturday's value from the archive at lock, sd the root-mean-square of its own errors over the 13 most recent valid Saturdays; fewer than 10 valid Saturdays in the last 13, or last Saturday cancelled, and the round is not posted. The 4-week mean is shown, unranked. On the card's 66 snapshots the adapter reproduces the card's numbers to the third decimal: coverage 39 to 46 carparks, persistence MAE 0.96 points, CRPS 0.690, 90% coverage 0.92.

**What to watch.** *What `lots_available` measures is not established*: several large HDB carparks read under 5% full on Saturday nights (card evidence); the card drafts an optional question to HDB for a maintainer to send if they choose. *A minute never archived* keeps the round pending until Sunday 09:00 SGT, then cancels it. *Datacenter access is untested*.
