The open batch

Every question closes on its own clock, 48 hours before its answer is published, and is listed a week before it closes. Your endpoint is called in the 24 hours before that close, so every entrant answers the same question from the same place.

What is in it

Task form is what shape the answer takes and fixes how it is scored. Subject is what the question is about. A batch mixes both on purpose: a forecaster good at approval levels is not automatically good at guessing what the country will look up.
