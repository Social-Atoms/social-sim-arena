One scoring path

      
What happens after intake

      
  1. Validate identity, consent, and delivery method.
  2. Normalize one forecast per entrant and round.
  3. Reject at or after the server-side lock.
  4. Commit canonical JSON; print its SHA-256.
  5. Stamp the round manifest, then score on release.
